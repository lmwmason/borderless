# LEGAL_DATA.md — Source and Legal Reliability Policy

## Scope
Only selected US federal and California topics for Korean B2B SaaS market entry. Candidate topics: foreign business activity/federal tax, state business registration, California privacy applicability, California sales/use tax, California employment registration. These are topics for review, not assertions of applicability.

## Authoritative starting points (verify exact pages and effective dates before use)
- US Small Business Administration: https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/register-your-business
- IRS international business: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses
- California Secretary of State: https://www.sos.ca.gov/business-programs/business-entities
- California Privacy Protection Agency: https://cppa.ca.gov/
- California Department of Tax and Fee Administration: https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov/
- California Employment Development Department: https://edd.ca.gov/

## Mandatory source record
`sourceId, agency, officialUrl, title, jurisdiction, effectiveFrom, reviewedAt, applicabilityConditions, exceptions, requiredFacts, excerptOrSection, verificationStatus`.

## Safety/accuracy policy
- Verify the actual official page and content; a homepage is a starting point, not a citation for a specific legal proposition.
- Never invent thresholds, citations, effective dates, case law or statutory text.
- Model cannot create arbitrary source IDs; findings must reference registry entries.
- Missing or conflicting facts => insufficient_data / review_required, not not_applicable.
- Explicitly state the checked topics and unexamined jurisdictions.
- Store rule and source version with each assessment. When the source is stale, mark review required.
- Distinguish regulatory obligation from optional business preference.
- Human reviewer approval required for consequential external actions and before commercial legal reliance.
